# FinRisk Intelligence Presentation Deck

## Slide 1 — Title

FinRisk Intelligence
Real-Time AI-Powered Financial Risk Intelligence & Stress Testing

## Slide 2 — Problem and Solution

- Financial events arrive in fragmented formats and sources
- Portfolio risk signals need to be evidence-based and explainable
- The solution fuses sentiment, event classification, and source credibility into a unified risk view

## Slide 3 — Architecture

- Data ingestion from RSS and demo sources
- NLP layer for sentiment and event classification
- Risk fusion and stress engine
- React dashboard with WebSocket-driven updates

## Slide 4 — AI/NLP Engine

- Entity extraction for firms, institutions, commodities, and regulators
- Event taxonomy mapping
- Sentiment score and confidence generation
- Explainability and novelty detection

## Slide 5 — Stress Testing and Dashboard

- Synthetic wholesale banking portfolio
- Scenario triggers such as geopolitical and credit stress
- Portfolio before/after calculations and asset-level impacts

## Slide 6 — Results and Domain Impact

- Demonstrated end-to-end analysis flow
- Live dashboard update without page refresh
- Practical risk monitoring use case for portfolio teams

## Slide 7 — Limitations and Future Work

- Prototype heuristic pipeline
- Need for better labeled financial datasets and model fine-tuning
- Broader live-data integration and stronger production controls
